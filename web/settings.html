Speech to text (STT)

Transcribes what you say. Scribe streams while you talk; the OpenRouter models get the whole clip on release. On-device models are free and run offline.

Pointing words

The words that mean “the thing under my cursor” (“this”, “that”, “here”…), per language. When you say one, Vizzo notes which element the cursor was on. Each language starts with a list you can change.

Text to speech (TTS)

Speaks the answers. On-device voices are free and run offline.

Traces

A record of every model call, kept on this Mac so a bad answer can be traced back: the prompt as it went to the harness (Vizzo’s instructions, the message with any history or notes added, which harness session it went to, and the launch settings) and the raw response. The harness’s own system prompt and the tool calls inside its turn aren’t in it: Vizzo only sees what goes in and comes out.
