Dashboard

What's set up now, and whether it works. Click a line to go to its section.

Models

Agents are kept warm for the chosen harness, model, level and speed. The checkboxes under a harness are what its agents load besides their file tools. Changes save at once and apply from the next turn.

Fast The router and the spoken progress lines, on a harness or a model API.
Smart The solver: hard questions and change requests, worked in the project folder with file tools.

Turns

Speech to text (STT)

Transcribes what you say. Scribe streams while you talk; the OpenRouter models get the whole clip on release. On-device models are free and run offline.

Text to speech (TTS)

Speaks the answers. On-device voices are free and run offline.

Keyboard

Click a shortcut, then press the new keys (Esc cancels). A modifier on its own works too: press it and let go. Changes save at once.
